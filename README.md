One line, under 200 characters: a funny autocorrect fail. The slip is one
clear wrong phrase ('I love you' for 'I will see you'), the victim is the
boss, and the punchline is the HR meeting it earns — autocorrect did not
just mistype, it promoted the sender.

## Entry

> I texted my boss 'I love you' instead of 'I will see you' — autocorrect just promoted me to HR's most awkward meeting of the year.

130 characters, one line, one sentence.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
# writes the entry to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt (default /output/entry.txt)
```

`main.ts` checks the entry before writing it: 1–200 characters, a single
line, exactly one sentence, and it must agree with `entry.txt` when that
file is present.

## Why this fail works

The joke needs exactly two ingredients: a recipient who must never get
that message (the boss) and a consequence that outlasts the typo (an HR
meeting). "Promoted me" turns the phone's mistake into a career event,
which is why the line lands instead of just reporting a typo.
